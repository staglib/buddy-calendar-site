Buddy — privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-14

Buddy is a personal, single-user application. It accesses Google Calendar only with the owner's consent, using OAuth.

Calendar data is used solely to answer the owner's requests and is stored only on the owner's own computer. Buddy does not sell, share, or transmit calendar data to third parties, and runs no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

OAuth tokens are stored locally with owner-only permissions and can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions page.
